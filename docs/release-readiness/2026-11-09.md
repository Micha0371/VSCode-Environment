### Completed custom-SMTP acceptance and startup hardening

A fresh first-signup email sent at 23:37 Berlin was opened on the connected
Android phone at 23:38. Metadata-only diagnostics showed a callback code, the
persisted PKCE verifier in the token request, and HTTP 200 from the session
exchange. Hosted Auth independently recorded confirmation and sign-in. The
protected empty pantry appeared without password login, survived force-stop and
relaunch, and normal logout returned to login. Exact fixture cleanup then reduced
Auth/profile/household/membership counts from four to three, left the one pantry
item unchanged, and verified deleted relations absent. The obsolete fixture
password was removed. Aggregate evidence is ignored at
`.verification/release-readiness/smtp-final-result.json`.

The successful attempt used the normal warm-app callback; its diagnostics did
**not** exercise the new cached-link fallback. Earlier failed cold starts led to
a deterministic regression against the installed Router's 150 ms Android
initial-URL timeout. Commit `85d0114` adds an Android root-startup fallback using
Expo's cached native URL, restricted to the configured confirmation route and
cleared after handoff. The delayed-bridge test fails before the change and passes
after it, with foreign-route/cache/platform checks. This establishes the covered
startup fix without claiming it caused the successful warm-app attempt.
